This quotation has been prepared based on the following conditions:
  • The moving vehicle has access to the area near the building entrance.
    (작업 차량이 건물 출입구 인근까지 진입 가능한 경우)
  • The moving work can be carried out during normal business hours on weekdays. (평일 정상 근무시간 내 작업이 가능한 경우)

    (일반적인 가정 이삿짐 기준)
CHAPTER III: Included Services
This quotation includes the following services:
  • Professional packing and transportation of household goods by container
    (포장 및 화물 컨테이너 운송)
  • Customs clearance services at both origin and destination
    (출발지 및 도착지 통관 업무)
  • Delivery of household goods into the residence under normal working conditions (일반적인 작업 환경에서의 자택 내 반입 작업)
  • Basic reassembly and placement of standard furniture (e.g. beds, dining tables, desks, and wardrobes)
    (침대, 식탁, 책상, 장롱 등 일반 가구의 기본 재조립 및 배치)
  • Placement of packed household items (such as clothing, books, and kitchenware) in the rooms designated by the customer
    (잔짐(의류, 서적, 주방용품 등)은 원하는 위치에 박스 배치)
  • Collection and disposal of packing materials on the day of delivery
    (작업 당일 포장재 및 폐기물 수거)
  • Return of the empty container (applicable to FCL shipments)
    (컨테이너 반납 업무 (FCL 화물 기준))

Unless otherwise specified, the following items are not included in this quotation:
  • Customs duties, Value Added Tax (VAT), GST, and any other applicable taxes

  • Costs related to customs inspections, X-ray inspections, quarantine inspections, and fumigation (세관 검사, X-ray 검사, 검역 검사, 훈증 관련 비용)
  • Demurrage, detention, and storage charges
    (Demurrage, Detention, Storage 비용)
  • Administrative fees such as parking permits and road occupancy permits
    (주차 허가증, 도로 점용 허가 등 행정 비용)
  • Long carry services (장거리 운반(Long Carry))
  • Stair carry services (계단 작업(Stair Carry))
  • Shuttle vehicle services (셔틀 차량(Shuttle Service))
  • Charges for special equipment such as ladder trucks or external lifts
    (사다리차, 외부 리프트 등 특수 장비 사용 비용)
  • Moving services above the 2nd floor where no elevator is available
    (엘리베이터가 없는 3층 이상 작업)
  • Assembly of IKEA furniture, flat-pack furniture, exercise equipment, or newly purchased furniture
    (IKEA 가구, 조립식 가구, 운동기구, 신규 구매 가구 조립)
  • Disconnection or reconnection of electricity, water, gas, and air-conditioning systems (전기, 수도, 가스, 에어컨 연결 및 철거 작업)
  • Installation of wall-mounted TVs, shelves, curtains, or similar fixtures
    (벽걸이 TV, 선반, 커튼 등 설치 작업)
  • Unpacking and organizing items inside boxes, wardrobes, closets, or kitchen cabinets (박스 내 물품의 수납 정리, 옷장, 주방장 내부 정리 서비스)
  • Handling of special heavy items such as pianos, safes, large exercise equipment, or machinery (피아노, 금고, 대형 운동기구, 기계류 등 특수 중량물 작업)
  • Additional services or extra deliveries not included in the quotation
    (추가 작업 또는 추가 배송)
  • Storage-in-Transit (SIT) services and related handling charges
    (창고 보관(SIT) 및 입출고 비용)
  • Insurance premium (보험료)
  • Weekend, public holiday, or after-hours services (주말, 공휴일 및 야간 작업)
